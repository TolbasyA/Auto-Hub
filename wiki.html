Car Wiki

A short guide to common car terms. Use it when reading listings or visiting a showroom.

Sedan

A car with a passenger cabin and a separate boot. Many sedans have four doors.

Hatchback

A car with a rear door that opens upward and gives access to the luggage area.

SUV

Sport utility vehicle. SUVs usually have a higher body than ordinary passenger cars. Not every SUV has all-wheel drive.

Transmission

The system that transfers power to the wheels. A manual gearbox requires the driver to select gears; an automatic changes gears automatically.

Electric vehicle

A battery-electric vehicle uses an electric motor and a rechargeable battery instead of a petrol or diesel engine.

Hybrid vehicle

A hybrid combines a combustion engine with an electric motor. Plug-in hybrids can also charge from an external power source.

Basic car care

  • Follow the maintenance schedule in the owner's manual.
  • Check tyre pressure and inspect tyres regularly.
  • Check the lights and wipers before a long journey.
  • Pay attention to warning lights and unusual sounds.
