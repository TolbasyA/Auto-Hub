Which type fits your needs?

Sedan

A separate boot and a comfortable cabin make a sedan a common choice for everyday trips.

SUV

A higher body and flexible luggage space can suit a family. Space varies by model.

Hatchback

A rear door opens into the luggage area. Compact hatchbacks are useful when parking space is limited.

Buying checklist

  1. Set a budget, including running costs.
  2. Check the car's documents and service history.
  3. Inspect the body, interior, tyres, and engine area.
  4. Arrange a test drive and an independent inspection.
